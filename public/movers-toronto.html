What to know about moving in Toronto

Condo and high-rise moves

Most Toronto towers need a booked elevator and set move-in hours. Tell us the building and we plan the crew size and timing around it.

Tight streets and parking

Many downtown and midtown streets are narrow, with limited room for a full-size truck. Flag it in your quote request so we plan the carry distance.

Older homes and stairs

Semis and rowhouses often mean steep stairs and narrow doorways. The estimator asks about stairs so the range reflects the real work.

Traffic-smart scheduling

The 401, the Gardiner and the DVP slow down at rush hour. We help you pick a start time that keeps the day short.

Questions about moving

How much does a Toronto move cost?

Local moves are billed by the hour with a crew, so the total depends on home size, stairs, elevator access and how far apart the two addresses are. Use the instant estimate for a tentative range, then we send a written quote within two hours.

Do you handle condo moves in Toronto?

Yes. Book the elevator and loading area with your building management first, and tell us the move-in window. We plan the crew around it.

Can you move me from Toronto to another city?

Yes. We run corridor and long-distance moves from Toronto across Ontario and Canada. The same estimator gives you a range for those routes too.
