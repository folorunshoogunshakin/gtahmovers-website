What to know about moving in Hamilton

Lower city and older homes

Many older Hamilton homes have steep stairs, narrow doorways and tight basements. The estimator asks about stairs so the range reflects the work.

Up and down the escarpment

Moves between the lower city and the Mountain mean routing a truck around the escarpment. We plan the route and timing for you.

Ancaster, Dundas and Stoney Creek

We cover all of Hamilton, including the surrounding communities. Add a street address in the estimator for a more accurate distance.

To and from the GTA

Hamilton to Toronto, Mississauga, Oakville and Burlington are regular routes for us, by the QEW, 403 and the Lincoln Alexander Parkway.
